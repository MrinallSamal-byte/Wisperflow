## 2. Video script (3 minutes max)

Record it yourself. Use your own words and your real numbers.

- **0:00 to 0:30, who you are.** [One sentence on you]. "I'm applying to Wispr Flow's India growth team, so instead of a resume I did the job for a week."
- **0:30 to 1:30, what I built.** Show the site. Show the funnel with your real numbers. Open the scorer, enter one creator, show the score and why. "I used this to decide who to pitch first." Show one real message and one real reply.
- **1:30 to 2:15, what voice changed.** Show a raw log entry. Be specific: [what did you dictate, how much faster or different was it, what did you notice about your own thinking].
- **2:15 to 2:45, what I got wrong.** Pick your best mistake from the log and what you changed.
- **2:45 to 3:00, what I'd do in the role.** [Two or three concrete next steps based on what replied].
